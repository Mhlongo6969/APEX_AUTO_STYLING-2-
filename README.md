## 📌 Project Overview
Apex Auto Styling & Mechanics is a comprehensive web solution built for an automotive customization and mechanical service business. The platform provides vehicle owners with detailed insights into available services—including vehicle wrapping, aesthetic body modifications, performance tuning, and routine mechanical maintenance. By offering clear navigation, service overviews, and interactive inquiry pages, the website addresses the real-world need for local auto shops to reach digital clientele, build brand credibility, and streamline customer service inquiries.

---

## 🎯 Website Goals and Objectives
* **Primary Goal**: To establish a professional online presence for Apex Auto Styling & Mechanics that boosts customer engagement and facilitates service requests.
* **Objective 1**: Deliver a fully responsive, user-friendly interface that operates seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
* **Objective 2**: Provide clear, accessible information regarding custom styling packages and mechanical service offerings.
* **Objective 3**: Implement a functional inquiry interface allowing prospective clients to submit service requests efficiently.

---

## ⚡ Key Features and Functionality
* **Feature 1: Interactive Multi-Page Navigation** – Clean and intuitive navigation header enabling smooth transitions across all site pages (`index.html`, `about.html`, `services.html`, `contact.html`, and `enquiry.html`).
* **Feature 2: Detailed Service Portfolio** – Itemized display of custom auto styling and mechanical repair options to assist users in selecting necessary services.
* **Feature 3: Dynamic Enquiry & Contact Forms** – Structured user contact and booking forms allowing customers to send direct service quotes and inquiries.
* **Feature 4: Advanced Responsive Layouts & Styling** – Extended Flexbox grids, media query adaptations, cards, interactive buttons, embedded maps, and media slots.
